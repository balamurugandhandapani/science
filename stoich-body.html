Goldschmidt Tolerance Factor

t = (rA + rO) / [√2 × (rB avg + rO)]

Stability guide: t = 0.9–1.0 ideal cubic perovskite · t = 0.71–0.9 orthorhombic/rhombohedral (tilted) · t > 1.0 hexagonal · t < 0.71 not perovskite-forming. Uses Shannon ionic radii for the relevant coordination number/oxidation state; rO is typically 1.40 Å (6-coordinate O2-).
