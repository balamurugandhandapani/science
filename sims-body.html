Photoelectric Effect

Below the threshold frequency no electrons come out however bright the light; above it, brightness changes how many (the current), while the colour sets their maximum energy. A negative collector voltage repels electrons until none arrive at . The current curve is an idealised straight-line model. Work functions are typical polycrystalline values (HyperPhysics, from the CRC Handbook) and vary by about 5–10% with surface condition.

Bragg Diffraction

Rays reflecting from neighbouring planes travel an extra path ; when that equals a whole number of wavelengths they add up and a strong peak appears. With more planes the peaks get sharper — the reason very small crystals give broad X-ray peaks (the Scherrer equation).

Radioactive Decay

Each atom decays at random, so a single run wobbles around the smooth dashed curve; with more atoms the run hugs the curve more closely. After one half-life about half of the atoms remain. Times here are in seconds for easy watching; real isotopes range from microseconds to billions of years.

Blackbody Spectrum

Try 5772 K (the Sun’s surface) — the peak sits in the visible band. Hotter objects peak at shorter wavelengths and radiate far more power. The classical curve climbs without limit at short wavelengths (the “ultraviolet catastrophe”) — Planck’s quantum idea fixes it. ; the colour swatch is an approximation.

Hydrogen Atom (Bohr Model)

When the electron drops from level to the atom emits a photon of exactly the energy difference. Jumps ending at are the visible Balmer lines. Uses the hydrogen Rydberg constant (reduced-mass corrected), giving vacuum wavelengths (Hα = 656.47 nm). Levels are drawn evenly spaced for clarity (real energies are listed beside them); the Bohr model is a teaching picture, not the full quantum atom.

DC Circuit (Ohm’s Law)

In series the same current flows through both resistors and the voltages add up to the battery voltage. In parallel both resistors have the full battery voltage and the currents add. Ideal battery and wires; the resistors are treated as ideal too.
